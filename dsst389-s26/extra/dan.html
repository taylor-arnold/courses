Disability and Personal Accomodations

[← back]

I am committed to helping all students succeed in this course, regardless of individual needs or circumstances. The course has been intentionally designed using principles of universal design, so that its default structures work well for a wide range of learners. Over the past several years, there has been a significant increase in disability accommodation requests. In the interest of clarity and fairness, I have outlined my responses to the most common situations below. Please feel free to reach out if you have questions, concerns, or additional accommodation needs that will help you do your best work in this class.

Extra Testing Time In-class exams are designed to require no more than 50 minutes of the 75-minute class period. Many students finish in 30 minutes or less. If you have an accommodation for extra time, I recommend starting the exam with the rest of the class. If additional time is needed, you may continue after class, either in the classroom or in my office.

Flexible Attendance and Assignments I strongly encourage all students to attend class and complete homework on schedule whenever possible, as this helps you get the most out of the course and avoid falling behind. If you have an accommodation for an increased number of excused absences, I will add five extra points to your baseline course participation grade. If flexibility with homework due dates is needed, please contact me directly so we can make appropriate arrangements.

Testing Space and Method Students with accommodations for alternative testing methods (for example, typing instead of handwriting, or testing in a quiet space) are encouraged to reserve space at the testing center if they feel it would be beneficial for an exam.

Notes and Recording Whenever note-taking is permitted, which is most of the time, all students may use laptops, tablets, or audio-recording devices for private use, regardless of whether they have formal accommodations through the university.

Availability of Materials All course materials, including solutions, are posted on the course website as soon as they are ready to be shared.

Personal Circumstances If serious personal or family issues arise during the semester, please contact me as soon as possible. We can work together to find a way for you to complete the course while also respecting the need to address circumstances outside of class.
