Pick three countries: (1) a country that starts with the same letter as your first name (if none exists, pick something close to the letter of your first name), (2) the farthest country away from Richmond that you have visited, and (3) the country you have the most interest in visiting but have not yet visited. Visit the epsg site and determine a coordinate system that would work for each of the three countries. Write down the EPSG number. If you picked a very large country, particularly one near the north or south pole (such as Russia or Canada), you can pick a coordinate system that's best for the capital city since there may not be one that works well for the whole country.
