Select a Wikipedia page of your choice and click on the "View history" tab on the top-right corner of the page. Write down the edit times of the last three most recent edits is ISO-8601 format (all times are in UTC). Try to manually compute the difference in some units of your choice (seconds, minutes, days) between the the first and second, and second and third edits and write those down.
