Image Project

[← back]

For today's task, we are going to build a large class dataset of images. Next week we will use these to finish our work learning how to build datasets and show an initial view of how to build predictive models. The task is fairly straightforward: you need to take 50 photos, consisting of 10 photos from each of the following catgories:

  • SetA: 10 photos taken outside with at least one hand in the foreground.
  • SetB: 10 photos with food items in the foreground. These must be 10 different food items.
  • SetC: 10 photos that show empty rooms inside of buildings that do not contain dogs, food, or hands. These must be from 10 different buildings.
  • SetD: select one object that you can carry around. Take 10 "artistic" photos of it in different locations across campus.
  • SetE: 10 photos taken outside of Roux 🐶. We will be walking around outside of the library and in front of the lake/THC.

Take your time and try to have fun with this! You can only take the photo with the dog when you've finished at least one of the other sets.

When you are done, create five folders in the Box folder I shared with you (it's the same one you'll use for the exam) named SetA, SetB, etc. with the ten photos from each category in appropriate directory. Any image format is fine. Make sure that your photos are not too similar from one another. The food and dog ones can be in the same place; the others should all be in different places. Please avoid having identifiable faces in the images. You are welcome to collect photos together in groups, but please take your own set of 50. If you have more than 10 in a category, feel free to include them!
