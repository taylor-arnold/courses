Final Project Instructions

[← back]

In this final project, you will apply the tools and methods you’ve learned in this course to perform an data analysis using the *Mixed Doubles Curling* dataset from the CSAS 2026 Data Challenge. Your goal is to explore, analyze, and model the data to uncover insights about strategy, performance, or outcomes in mixed doubles curling. You may address one of the suggested research prompts from the CSAS challenge, or develop your own unique research question inspired by the data. There is a starting template file that you can use to read the data into Python through Google Colab.

Format: Your project will be presented as a short 10-minute presentation, using whatever slides format you prefer (Google, Powerpoint, PDF). This will be followed by a follow-up discussion about your analysis. You do not need to submit the code you used, but please email me a copy of the slides immediately after your presentation. You may work alone or in a group of 2-3 people. Please determine the groups that you want to work with (or not) by Monday, November 3rd.

Components: In order to receive full credit for the project, your analysis should apply data from at least three of the tables, show at least three significantly different visualizations of the data, and present one statistical inference test. Everyone in the group should be able to explain all elements of the final project.

Schedule: Each group will be assigned a 15 or 20 minute time slot (depending on the group sizes) during one of the two class meeting times during the final week of the semester. The presentations and follow-up questions will be done over Zoom and will done privately with each group. We will not meet in person as a whole class during the final.
