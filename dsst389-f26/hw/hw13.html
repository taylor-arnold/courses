This reading runs the same pipeline over the French Wikipedia pages for the same 75 authors. Hand write answers to the following.

  1. Comparing part-of-speech proportions between the two editions shows the French pages using more determiners. The reading says this difference is linguistic rather than editorial. Explain the distinction, and give an analysis that would reach a false conclusion about French and English Wikipedia by failing to look past it.
  2. The English and French document vectors cannot be compared to each other, but the reading says the structure of each collection can be. Say what makes the vectors incomparable, in terms of what the axes of each space are. Then say what it is that you are actually comparing when you put the two UMAP plots side by side.
  3. Suppose the French projection groups the authors differently than the English one. The reading suggests this reflects a real difference in how two communities of editors describe these figures. Before accepting that, give two other explanations for the difference that have nothing to do with editors, and say what you would check to rule each one out.
