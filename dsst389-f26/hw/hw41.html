The reading introduces TF-IDF using four short phrases, and the first two questions ask you to work that example by hand. Here they are again. Bring a calculator; use natural logs, as the reading does.

1. chop the onion and garlic
2. chop chop chop the garlic
3. sauté the garlic in olive oil
4. add the onion and olive oil to the pan

  1. Write out the term-frequency matrix for these four phrases, one row per phrase and one column per distinct term, with raw counts in the cells.
  2. Using the smoothed formula from the reading, compute the TF-IDF score of "chop", "the", and "garlic" in phrase 2, and rank the terms of phrase 2 by their scores.
  3. Say why "the" still comes out with a score above zero, and what setting max_df=0.5 would have done to it instead.
  4. Say what causes the top TF-IDF terms to be names and places where the top raw counts were words like "year" and "work".
  5. Say whether TF-IDF is a property of a word, of a document, or of a collection.
