The first three questions use the small undirected network given by the edge list below. Draw it on your paper first, then compute the metrics by hand. Put the nodes wherever they look clearest; the drawing is a tool, not an answer.

A–B, A–C, B–C, C–D, D–E, E–F, E–G, F–G

  1. Draw the network, write the degree of every node, and say how many components it has.
  2. Compute the closeness centrality of A and of D from their shortest-path distances to every other node.
  3. Find the node that lies on every shortest path between the two triangles, and explain how betweenness can rank it first while its degree is among the lowest in the network.
  4. Split your drawing into two clusters and say which cluster node D belongs in and why the answer is not obvious.
  5. Say what an edge in the Wikipedia network actually records, given that no author in the corpus ever linked to anyone.
