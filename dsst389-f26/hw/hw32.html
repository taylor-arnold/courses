The reading loads state from a GeoJSON file. It has fifty rows, one per state, and only three columns of attributes: name, abb, and fips. Everything else in the table is geometry. On a sheet of paper, hand write answers to the following.

  1. Explain why the distortion from projection is easier to see in the state polygons than in the metro points from the last reading.
  2. Say what is actually wrong with a centroid or an area computed in raw longitude and latitude.
  3. Name a state whose centroid falls outside its own borders, and say what that should make you cautious about when centroids stand in for states.
  4. Name one variable you would want to join onto state, and say whether it should be a count or a rate and why.
  5. Say why a file format would store a single-part shape like Colorado as a MultiPolygon anyway.
