The reading uses metro, a dataset with one row for each metropolitan statistical area in the United States and columns for population, economic indicators, and the coordinates lon and lat. On a sheet of paper, hand write answers to the following and bring them to class.

  1. State the two objections the reading raises to plotting raw lat against lon, and say which one is about accuracy and which is about use.
  2. Explain what the number 4326 records, and why the string POINT (-86.8 32.4) on its own does not identify a place on Earth.
  3. Give one analysis of metro that comes out wrong if you work in EPSG:4326, and say whether its distances come out too large or too small.
  4. Explain in your own words why Web Mercator (EPSG:3857) makes Greenland look bigger than Africa.
  5. Name one question about metro areas that the single-point simplification can still answer honestly, and one for which it would quietly mislead you.
