This reading starts building the Wikipedia corpus that the next several chapters all analyze. The population is the set of articles tagged with two categories, Category:Victorian novelists and Category:19th-century Irish novelists, which comes out to 376 authors. On a sheet of paper, hand write answers to the following.

  1. Explain what makes a 404 response worth caching and a 429 worth forgetting.
  2. Describe the kind of author who ends up missing from a corpus defined by Wikipedia category tags, and say whether the omissions are likely to be random.
  3. Say what page_id and doc_id each can do that the other cannot, and which of the two is the primary key.
  4. Say why a title that resolves to no article at all should be looked at rather than dropped.
  5. Say what makes it safe to discard fields while flattening the API's nested JSON here.
