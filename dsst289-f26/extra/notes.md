# 2025-10-15

  - Question: What is your experience with statistical inference?
  - Go through the notes for Chapter 5 in class.
  - Exercise where each table (or side of a table) has to build a schema for a task and then present it.

    - Music Playlists
      - Collect information about playlists from students on Spotify. What makes a song popular across different students' playlists? Are certain artists or genres consistently showing up, or do we all listen to completely different music?

    - Campus Dining Locations
      - Do different dining locations on campus have similar prices? Does this depend on the type of thing you are buying for, for example breakfast, 
      snack, lunch, or drinks?
      - What counts as a "similar item" (e.g., all sandwiches, all breakfast items)?

    - Student Schedule Mapping
      - What are the most popular courses and time slots among students in this class? Are there common scheduling conflicts we could identify? What
      subjects are the most popular?
      
    - Campus Classroom Accessibility
      - How accessible are the main academic buildings on campus for students with mobility needs? Which buildings should be prioritized for accessibility improvements? Does access to the buildings depend on the time of day?

    - Daily Caffeine Consumption
      - How much caffeine do students in this class consume throughout a typical day, and when during the day are we most likely to reach for coffee, tea, or energy drinks?

    - Study Location Preferences
      - Where do students in this class prefer to study throughout the week, and how do study locations relate to the type of work being done and time of day?
